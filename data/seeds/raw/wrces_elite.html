Best Countries in Sport 2026

World Ranking of Countries in Elite Sport (WRCES)

Which countries are the best in sport? The World Ranking of Countries in Elite Sport (WRCES) is the first research-based index to answer that scientifically — ranking the 206 countries with National Olympic Committees across every sport, Olympics and non-Olympics.

Unlike the Olympic medal table, the WRCES weights each sport by its level of competition, giving a true picture of national sporting performance.

Frequently asked questions

Which country is the best at sport?

In the 2026 World Ranking of Countries in Elite Sport (WRCES), USA ranks as the best country in sport.

How are countries ranked in sport?

The WRCES scores every country across all sports recognised by SportAccord, weighting each sport by how popular and universal it is — so success in widely practised, widely followed sports counts for more.
